Who We Are

Craftsmanship shaped by family experience

True Craft Interiors grew from a construction family. Our father introduced us to job sites at a young age, where we learned discipline, clean work habits and why every detail matters before the final coat.

That foundation became the way we work today: licensed, fully insured and backed by over 25 years of hands-on experience, more than 40 years of family drywall knowledge and a 1-year workmanship warranty.

What motivates us most is seeing each client's project come to life with a finish that improves the space and exceeds expectations.

Our Mission

To transform spaces with durable craftsmanship, functional finishes, honest communication and results that feel finished correctly from the inside out.

Our Vision

To become a trusted leader in carpentry and remodeling, recognized for craftsmanship, integrity, outstanding service and lasting client relationships.

  • Clean, detailed finishes using trusted materials.
  • Integrity, respect and honest communication.
  • Reliable schedules, prompt finishes and clean work areas.
  • Customer satisfaction, excellence and lasting relationships.
  • New ideas, proven techniques and quality materials.
  • Residential, commercial and remodeling partner work.
